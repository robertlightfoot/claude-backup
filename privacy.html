Privacy policy

Claude Backup is a personal backup tool used only by its owner, Rob Lightfoot.

  • It uses the Google Drive drive.file permission, so it can only see files it created itself, not anything else in Google Drive.
  • Backups are encrypted on the owner's computer before upload and stored only in the owner's own Google Drive.
  • No data is collected, sold, shared with third parties or used for any other purpose.
